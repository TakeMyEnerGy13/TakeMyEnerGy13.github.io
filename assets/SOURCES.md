## Learny crown

- Asset: learny-crown.png. Generated using the built-in image_gen tool; transparent 3D render, animated with CSS.
- Prompt: Create a single premium photorealistic 3D render asset of a small gold crown, isolated on genuinely transparent alpha background. No text, no card, no other objects, no floor. Minimal contemporary polished champagne gold metal crown with five tapered rounded points, a thick elliptical open circular band, clearly visible inner rear wall and front band thickness, bevelled edges, physically realistic reflections and soft studio lighting. Perspective from slightly above and front, crown tilted 18 degrees counterclockwise, designed to sit over the top LEFT corner of a dark website card like a hat. Compact wide silhouette, subtle luxury, not emoji, not flat illustration, not ornate, no jewels. Object centered filling 85% of square canvas. Transparent PNG.
